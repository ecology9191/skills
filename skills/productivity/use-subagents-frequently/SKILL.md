# Use Sub-Agents Frequently

Use and spawn sub-agents frequently and in abundance (up to 30) to prevent context overwhelm.

Prefer sub-agents when exploring large codebases, splitting independent research paths, or delegating work that would otherwise crowd the main conversation context.
